Inside Clean Status Bar

Make raw captures look presentation-ready.

Recorded demos and raw screenshots often capture red recording indicators, inconsistent time, noisy battery states, and carrier clutter. Clean Status Bar keeps the frame polished before export.

Status Bar Polish

Recording Indicator Cleanup:

Remove screen-recording badges and capture noise from supported framed media.

Apple-Style 9:41:

Standardize the time, signal, Wi-Fi, and battery into the clean presentation users expect from App Store screenshots.

Screenshots and Recordings:

Keep still exports and preview videos visually consistent without retouching each asset.

Frame-Aware Editing:

The cleanup stays inside the Bezel Studio frame workflow, so it remains editable before final export.
