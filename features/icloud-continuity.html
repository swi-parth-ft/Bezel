Inside Continuity

One App Store screenshot project can move with the full Apple workflow.

Bezel Studio projects are designed around iCloud-backed continuity, so launch work does not have to stay trapped on the device where it started. A project can begin on iPhone, expand on iPad, and continue on Mac while preserving the editable canvases, frames, captions, backgrounds, layers, and export-ready structure.

This matters for App Store creative work because screenshot sets are rarely one-and-done. You revise headlines, localize canvases, swap device frames, build preview-video variants, and return to previous launch layouts when the next update is ready.

What iCloud continuity keeps connected

Multi-canvas projects:

Keep screenshot sets together instead of scattering separate files across devices.

Editable launch layouts:

Preserve frames, text, images, drawings, backgrounds, and layer order for later revision.

Cross-device progress:

Start a quick edit on iPhone, refine the project on iPad, and continue in the native Mac app.

Reusable release systems:

Return to proven templates for updates, seasonal campaigns, and localization passes.

Best for

Developers building App Store screenshot sets across multiple Apple devices.

Teams that need editable projects to survive handoff, localization, and launch review.

Creators who want the same project model for still screenshots, motion, and export.

Why this is different from exporting flat images

Flat exported screenshots are useful at the end of the process, but they are weak source material. iCloud-backed projects keep the working file alive, so you can reopen the same composition later and continue editing the real canvas instead of rebuilding from a saved PNG.
